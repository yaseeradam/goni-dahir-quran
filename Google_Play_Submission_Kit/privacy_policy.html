Privacy Policy for Goni Dahir Quran Audio

Effective Date: November 7, 2026

Developer: FrontalMinds Nigeria (princeykarg@gmail.com)

This Privacy Policy describes how Goni Dahir Quran Audio ("we", "our", or "the application") handles user data. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and providing a safe, advertisement-free, and respectful religious audio experience.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Goni Dahir Quran Audio is built with a strict privacy-first architecture:

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for, collect, store, or transmit your name, phone number, email address, physical address, or contacts.
  • No User Accounts: The application does not require any account registration, login, or authentication.
  • No Location Tracking: We do not track or access your GPS or physical location data.
  • No Advertising Identifiers: The application does not contain advertisements, advertising SDKs, or tracking cookies.

2. Permissions Used by the Application

The application requests only the essential permissions required to deliver audio playback functionality:

  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Used exclusively to stream Surah audio files from our high-speed Content Delivery Network (CDN) and download Surahs for offline listening upon explicit user request.
  • Foreground Service & Media Playback (android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): Used to keep audio recitations playing continuously in the background when your screen is locked or while multitasking.
  • Wake Lock (android.permission.WAKE_LOCK): Prevents your phone CPU from entering sleep mode while you are actively listening to recitation.
  • Post Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): On Android 13+, this permission enables playback controls (Play, Pause, Skip) in the notification drawer and lock screen.

3. Local Device Storage & Caching

When you choose to download a Surah for offline playback, the audio file is stored locally in your device's private app directory. This data remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded or shared with any third party. You can delete individual Surahs or clear the entire offline cache at any time within the app's Settings.

4. Third-Party Services

Audio files and catalog metadata are fetched securely via HTTPS from our dedicated media servers (https://quran.academyhub.com.ng). Server access logs (standard HTTP requests including IP address and user-agent) are kept strictly for infrastructure performance, caching, and DDOS mitigation and are never used to track individual users.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application contains purely Islamic Quran recitation content and is safe for users of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal data whatsoever, we comply fully with the requirements of the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy frameworks.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.
